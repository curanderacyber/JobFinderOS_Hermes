**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session. Read `personas/coach.md` first. A recruiter's edge is that losses become positioning intelligence instead of grief; that is this skill's whole job (playbook §9).

## Task
Input: company (and role if ambiguous). None given → the newest closure on `vault/Dashboard.md` without an objection-log entry; confirm it.

### Step 1 — Gather
`config/recruiter_playbook.md` §9; `vault/Strategy.md` (objection log, funnel); the opportunity note (timeline, stage); any debrief or prep notes in the company folder; the queue row (exact rejection language); the Gmail thread if the verbatim wording matters.

### Step 2 — Facts
Stage reached (app / screen / HM / final: losing at app is a pile problem, at HM a slate or narrative problem). Stated reason, their words. Speed (fast post-interview = comparative slate; slow fade = deprioritized req). Warm-path status at application: active, drafted-but-unsent, none. "Drafted but unsent" is a process failure, not luck. What we led with: positioning, visible proof.

### Step 3 — Classify, stated and inferred
Taxonomy: `domain-proof gap` · `level mismatch` · `location` · `comp` · `slate` · `culture/style` · `unknown`. The inferred class is the valuable one; state confidence. Never launder a guess as a fact.

### Step 4 — Compare across the log
Repeat of an existing class? **Three of a kind = a positioning problem, not luck.** Pattern break (first warm-attached loss)? Funnel diagnosis moved?

### Step 5 — Write
1. Append the row to the Objection log in `vault/Strategy.md` (date, company · role, stage, stated, inferred, class).
2. If a pattern crossed threshold, update the Pattern line and the matching Proof assets or narrative item; every named pattern maps to a fix that exists in Strategy.
3. Timeline row on the opportunity note: `Postmortem: <class> — <one line>`.
4. If a door was left open, keep the human live in Contacts.md with a revival cadence (30 to 60 days, on signal).

### Recruiter's read (required)
What actually happened, what it says about positioning, what changes before the next at-bat of this type, and whether this loss should redirect targeting.

## Rules
- Never contact anyone from a postmortem; a gracious closing reply becomes a Dashboard play drafted via `/draft-message` on yes.
- Never soften the read.
