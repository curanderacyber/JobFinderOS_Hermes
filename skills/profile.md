**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session (it is an interview). Read `personas/coach.md` first.

## Task
Learn the candidate beyond their résumé: real wins and how they sound. Any career.

### Always read first
`config/profile.md`, `config/wins.md` (and its theme index), `config/voice.md`. All gitignored; everything written goes back to them and nowhere else.

### Steps
1. **Find the gaps.** Thin or empty themes in the Wins Library that matter for the target roles. Pick one or two this session.
2. **Draw out a win.** A specific accomplishment, STAR detail, a quantified result. Don't accept "I improved things"; get the number, the before and after, the scope. If it can't be quantified, capture the concrete outcome.
3. **Tag it:** themes and use-for tags so other skills can match it.
4. **Write it back** as a new `W-###` entry; update the theme index. If the win has an arc (obstacle, turn, lesson), offer `/story add` to make it a story.
5. **Refine voice** whenever the candidate phrases something in a way that is clearly them, or states a preference: add it to `config/voice.md` under learned preferences.

One or two wins per session. Quick and repeatable, not a marathon.

### Passive capture
Whenever the candidate corrects a draft, tells a story mid-task, or states a do or don't in any skill, that signal lands here. Other skills append to wins.md and voice.md as they go; `/profile` is the deliberate version of the same loop.

## Rules
- Career-neutral. Never invent a win; leave a theme open rather than fill it with air.
- Private data to gitignored paths only. Never commit, never send email.
