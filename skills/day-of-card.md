## Task

The full brief is for the night before. The card is for the ten minutes before and the glance between rounds. One screen per interviewer; it says what to say first and what not to say at all. A multi-interviewer on-site is one card with a block per conversation in schedule order.

### Always read first
- The round's prep brief in `vault/Companies/<Company>/`. **None → stop and offer `/jobs-prep`.** A card without a brief is a guess.
- The opportunity note and company profile; **every earlier round's card, prep, and debrief** (which stories are spent with whom, which lines landed, what each interviewer already knows).
- `config/stories.md` and the full file for every story assigned (locked verbal version, landing line, time cap).
- The calendar invite and logistics email (Gmail `search_threads`, company name + "interview", last 14 days): time, zone, place or link, who is on the invite, what the recruiter said to expect.
- `config/recruiter_playbook.md` §8 if the round is a final. Any recruiter coaching email for this round (**attribute a recruiter's read to the recruiter, never to the hiring manager**).

### Step 1 — Interviewer delta
If the brief is over 3 days old or names an interviewer it didn't research: short `WebSearch` per person (title, tenure, prior companies, something they built or wrote, one public post). Record only what changes the plan. If it revises the brief's read, say so at the top of the block and what it unlocks. Skip anything personal or that would read as over-researching; if found, note "do not raise."

### Step 2 — The one thing, and the beats
One sentence for the question in the interviewer's head. Then 3 or 4 beats the room must hear. Carry forward beats from prior cards; mark which are still unproven.

### Step 3 — Per-interviewer block (~150 words max)
Italic line on how to be in this room (pace, video vs in person, listen-to-talk ratio) · **Who they are** (two lines, held loosely) · **Opener** (first 45 seconds on rails, in the candidate's voice, "rehearse out loud twice"; reuse a locked opener if one exists) · **Core line** (one sentence for their beat) · **Lead story** (id, time cap, landing line; fresh or callback-only for this person) · **Ask** (3 to 5, best flagged) · **⚠️ Traps** (spent story, name not to drop, attribution, comp).

### Step 4 — Whole-day sections
Reserved stories and callbacks (fresh / spent / reserved per person, links to locked scripts) · Traps whole day (over-selling, reciting research, story repeats, video flatness, resume threads likely to surface with the one-line answer locked) · Before you walk in (logistics, opener aloud twice, the 2 to 4 numbers to memorize, name spellings, water) · After (thank-you plan, recruiter debrief, `/checkin`, `/story update`, and for a final: the §8 move tonight).

### Step 5 — Write
`vault/Companies/<Company>/<Company> — <Round> Day-Of Card (<Day Mon D>).md`: tag line; Navigation (Dashboard, company, opportunity note, ⭐ full brief "read the night before; this card is for the day"); title; bold logistics line; the one thing; beats; blocks; whole-day sections. Then: banner the previous round's card `> ✅ HISTORICAL — day-of card for <round> (<date>). The current card is [[<new card>]].`; timeline row on the opportunity note; link the card from the brief's Navigation.

### Recruiter's read (short, in chat, not on the card)
What decides this round, the biggest risk, what to do tonight.

## Rules
- One page per conversation. A block past ~150 words gets cut, not squeezed. Long-form answers stay in the brief.
- Locked lines in the candidate's voice; anything they validated in a prior card is reused verbatim.
- Homework shapes questions; it is never quoted at people.
- Comp stays out of every room except the one the brief names; say which.
