**Agent:** `coach` · runs in this session. Read `personas/coach.md` first (§3 authenticity doctrine is the law here).

## Task
Input: a company (`/network-outreach <Company>`) or "all Tier 1" to run across the profile's Tier 1 list one company at a time, pausing after each. Invoking with a target is the candidate's yes to draft. If no input, ask which company.

### Always read first
`config/profile.md` (background, strengths, tiers, career history for overlap), the company profile (existing contacts and communication log), `vault/Tracking/Contacts.md`, and the opportunity note if a req is live.

### Step 1 — Audit and cap check
Note anyone already in active outreach or warm status; never re-draft for a contact messaged in the past 30 days. **Hard gate:** an unanswered thread at this company means STOP; report its state and the date a second thread is allowed. If 10 new humans were contacted this week, bank the play with a "ready when the week resets" note.

### Step 2 — Find 2 to 3 humans
`WebSearch` for peer-level or one-level-above people, in this order: the hiring manager for the live role and their boss (log both in the opportunity note's Key Contacts, playbook §7); the leader of the candidate's function; the leader of an adjacent function; a technical recruiting lead only if no functional leader is visible. Avoid CEO/CTO without a documented warm angle, generic recruiters, and anyone already contacted. For each: name, exact title, LinkedIn URL, tenure, any public signal (posts, talks, writing, announcements). Draft for the best fit; log the others as secondary.

### Step 3 — Research the hook
Find a specific, verifiable reason to write: a visible business challenge, something they personally wrote or said, a shared background, a recent milestone. **Two-fact rule enforced:** (a) one fact that took real work to find, source URL listed in Hook Research; (b) a `[YOUR TAKE]` slot only the candidate can fill. If research produces no genuine hook, say so and recommend not sending. Never fabricate familiarity.

### Step 4 — Draft
Structure: one sentence of hook; one sentence of credibility (specific: current title and employer from the profile); optional commitment signal (applied last week); a small ask (15 minutes, or a genuine question); sign-off with the short name. Max 5 sentences. No "came across your profile," no "exploring opportunities," no unspecific flattery, no bullets. InMail subject: the hook in 5 to 7 words.

Variants: cold peer (problem or observation, credibility, 15 minutes) · milestone (one detail proving the reading, a question about what it unlocks) · post-connect warm-up (thanks, one observation or question, no ask) · bump at 7 to 10 days (one sentence, new angle, no re-explaining, no apology).

### Step 5 — Cadence
| Step | Timing | Action |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Send |
| 2 | Day 7 to 10 if silent | One-sentence bump, new angle |
| 3 | On connect | Post-connect warm-up |
| 4 | Day 14 if still silent | Rest; move to Warm — Monitor |

### Step 6 — Write
- `vault/Outreach Drafts/<Company> — Network — <Name>.md`: type, to, LinkedIn, status Draft, date; the message with its `[YOUR TAKE]` slot; a **Before you send** checklist (read the hook source, fill the slot, rewrite one sentence in your own words, verify claims, send at a human time); the cadence table; Hook Research with URLs; secondary contacts.
- `vault/Tracking/Contacts.md`: primary under Active Outreach (warmth cold, last touch = draft date, next touch = bump date, goal, cadence); secondaries plus HM and boss under Priority — Not Yet Contacted.
- Company profile communication log: draft written, not sent.

### Step 7 — Report
Who and why, the draft plus checklist, the cadence, the secondaries logged, files touched, and the **Recruiter's read**: where this thread fits in the company play (thread 1 of 2, the pivot name and date, when the application should follow).

## Rules
- Never write as if the candidate is job searching; the frame is peer curiosity.
- Only facts WebSearch confirms.
